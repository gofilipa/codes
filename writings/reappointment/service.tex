\subsection{External}
\label{sec:org66bfdca}
In addition to my work at Pratt, I also serve as a reviewer for two
scholarly publications, one in the Humanities, the \emph{\href{https://read.dukeupress.edu/social-text}{Social Text}}
journal and another in Computer Science, the annual \href{https://gebnlp-workshop.github.io/}{Workshop on Gender
Bias in Natural Language Processing} (GeBNLP).

Published by Duke University Press, \emph{Social Text} is leading,
peer-reviewed, and radically-oriented journal in cultural studies,
which consistently publishes provocative work on gender, sexuality,
race, postcolonialism, and popular culture. I have been a member of
the journal's editorial board for two years, regularly attending
meetings and reviewing submissions.

GeBNLP is an annual workshop that takes place across different venues.
Last year, I published my paper, "Some Myths About Bias: A Queer
Studies Reading Of Gender Bias In NLP" their \emph{Proceedings of the 6th
Workshop on Gender Bias in Natural Language Processing (GeBNLP)} at
the Association for Computational Linguistics. This year, I have
joined the workshop as a reviewer for the 7th Workshop on Gender Bias
in Natural Language Processing at AACL-IJCNLP 2026.
