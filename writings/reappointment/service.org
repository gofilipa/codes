This service statement covers my service work at the Pratt Institute, School of
Information (SI), at Pratt Institute broadly, and my external service. 

** Pratt SI
Since beginning my position at Pratt School of Information (SI), I
have been involved in faculty committees and taskforces, student
groups, and in the faculty union. I have also been a faculty advisor
to ~30 students per semester, and ran workshops for the Digital
Storytelling Lab in AY 2025-2026 and 2026-2027.

At Pratt SI, I have worked on curriculum development, including the DH
Certificate Review, which went through multiple rounds of revision
over the last academic year, on the Ethics and Technology Forum
Committee, the MS Critical and Creative Computing Task Force, and the
DEI Committee. Additionally, I have served on two faculty searches,
the Pratt Faculty Council, and the Peer Review Committee.

I have also served as a Faculty Advisor to two student groups, Latines
in Tech, and the /Stacks/ Student Journal (see my enclosed Teaching
Statement for more information on my role in these groups).
Additionally, I have spoken at an event, Digital Spring Clean Up Day,
organized by student the groups PALA, PEMP, SAA, and Patients and
Pixels.

Below is a summary my service at Pratt SI: 
- Faculty committee, taskforce, and miscellenious work:

  - MS Critical and Creative Computing Task Force (2026-2027)
  - Ethics and Technology Forum Committee (Summer 2026)
  - DH Certification Review (2025-2027)
  - DEI Committee (2025-2027)
  - Faculty Search Committee (2024-2026)

- Involvement with student groups:
  - Faculty Advisor, /Stacks/ Student Journal (2025-2027)

  - Speaker, Digital Spring Clean-Up Day event (Spring 2026)

** Pratt Institute
Beyond SI, I have also been involved in the Pratt Faculty Union,
UFCT 1460. Primarily, I work as a member of the Data Team, helping the
union to process and migrate union data to a new system. A full list
of my tasks and roles for the union includes the following:
- Member of the Data Team (2026-2027)
- Union delegate for School of Information (2025-2027)
- Participant in the Summer 2026 membership drive (2025-2026)
- Participant in "Organizing For Power" training series (2025-2026)

** External
In addition to my work at Pratt, I also serve as a reviewer for two
publications: /[[https://read.dukeupress.edu/social-text][Social Text]]/, published by Duke University Press, and
the [[https://gebnlp-workshop.github.io/][Workshop on Gender Bias in Natural Language Processing]] (GeBNLP),
held annually at different conference venues. My goal with these
service obligations is to develop and maintain my committment to
Humanities and Computer Science scholarly communities.

Known for its radical and activist leaning, /Social Text/ is a leading
peer-reviewed journal in cultural studies, one that has published
consistently provocative work on gender, sexuality, race, colonialism,
environmental issues, and popular culture for nearly 50 years. I have
been a member of the journal's editorial board for three years,
regularly attenting their quarterly meetings and reviewing submissions
several times per year.

GeBNLP is an annual workshop that takes place every year for the past
6 years. At last year's conference, held at the Association for
Computational Linguistics (ACL) in Vienna, I presented my paper, "Some
Myths About Bias: A Queer Studies Reading Of Gender Bias In NLP" in
their /Proceedings of the 6th Workshop on Gender Bias in Natural
Language Processing (GeBNLP)/. For this year's workshop, taking place
at AACL-IJCNLP 2026, I am a reviewer. I plan to continue as both a
reviewer and contributer to the workshop in future years.
