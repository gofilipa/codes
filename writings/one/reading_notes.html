BOSTOCK V. CLAYTON COUNTY (2020)

Gerald Bostock, a gay man, began working for Clayton County, Georgia, in child welfare. In 2013, Bostock began participating in a gay recreational softball league. Shortly thereafter, Bostock received criticism for his participation in the league and for his sexual orientation and identity generally. Shortly afterwards, Clayton County terminated Bostock allegedly for “conduct unbecoming of its employees.”

Court held that Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 protects employees against discrimination because they are gay or transgender.
