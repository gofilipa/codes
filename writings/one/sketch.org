*** chapter argument
Text capture technology can be worked into reparative motives.

In text capture, language data is processed into objects of commercial
value. This extraction is exhaustive and degrades potential meaning.

How can we re-work text capture practices to resist this total
degradation of meaning?
- Text capture is driven by protocol, which can be re-worked toward
  reparative motives.
- Slowing down the process and deliberately choosing our datasets.
- reading for vulnerability, reading for what is exposed, like fear.

*** technical constraint: protocol
What is the technical constraint proper to web scraping?
- protocol, the code for moving data across web servers
- web scraping is a way of miming browser requests

How is protocol being re-worked in this chapter?
- drawing from Sedgwick's theorization of paranoid vs reparative
  reading.
  - protocol in its current usage follows a logic of scale that
    mirrors paranoid logics. Turning to a reparative framework allows
    protocol to tend to what might be generative even within
    repression.
- protocol is being used to scrape small and highly specific datasets.
